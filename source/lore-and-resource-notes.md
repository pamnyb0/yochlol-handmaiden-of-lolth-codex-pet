# Lore and source notes

- The AD&D 2nd Edition Yochlol is known as a handmaiden of Lolth; the amorphous form has one eye and eight pseudopods. A concise transcription and bibliographic trail are collected by the [Complete Compendium](https://www.completecompendium.com/appendix/yochlolu/).
- In *Menzoberranzan* (1994), the supplied walkthrough identifies Azarell as a Yochlol in disguise and describes both her reveal and later betrayal. See the [encounter notes and screenshots](https://www.swordsandsoftware.com/menzo.php).
- The user-supplied resource audit names YOCHOL, graphics record 102, and RES3 slots 102–135 in NPCS.DAT. We preserve that provenance without claiming to have decoded the original image frames.
- The user supplied the miniature photograph as an identity reference. The finished sprite uses its squat pooled mass, high red eye, enormous vertical mouth and uneven pseudopods. The photo is kept as a reference asset and is not part of the install package.
